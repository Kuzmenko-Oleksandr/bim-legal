Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 August 2026

BILLIONS IN A MINUTE (“the app”, “we”) is a personal automation tool that publishes short business-education videos to social platforms on behalf of its owner. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

What we access

  • Account authorization. When you connect a TikTok or YouTube account, we receive an OAuth access token and refresh token that let the app upload videos to that same account. We never receive your password.
  • Basic account info. We may read your channel/profile id and the upload/processing status of videos we create, only to record which video was posted.

What we do not do

  • We never read your private messages, followers, or other users’ data.
  • We never post to any account other than the authenticated owner’s own account.
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

Storage & retention

Tokens and video metadata are stored locally on the owner’s own machine/server (an encrypted .env file and a local SQLite database). They are kept only as long as needed to operate the app and can be deleted at any time by revoking access.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app’s access at any time. TikTok: in the TikTok app under Security & permissions. Google/YouTube: at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Once revoked, the tokens stop working immediately.

Content

Videos are original, factual business-history content produced by the owner’s pipeline using licensed stock footage (Pexels), synthetic narration and on-screen captions.
